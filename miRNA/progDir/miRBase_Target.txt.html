Comments

Two groups independently reported miRs derived from the same precursor. Lagos-Quintana et al. [1] reported miR-17 expressed from the 3' arm of the hairpin precursor in HeLa cells. The presence of the mature miR was confirmed by Northern blotting, abut the extents of the excised sequence are unknown. Mourelatos et al. [2] reported cloning miR-91 from the 5' arm of the same precursor. This sequence is renamed miR-17-5p here.
