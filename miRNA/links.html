Bioinformatics Tools


  Here are some bioinformatics tools that I have developed. Feel free to use them and your comments will be greatly appreciated. 

   WebTools
     * LOD Score Calculator for Sequence Modeling
     * Pairwise Protein Sequence Alignment  (Using multiple substitution scoring matrixes)
     * Simple Sequence Alignment (Using simple scoring scheme match/mismatch/gap)
     * Sequence Fitting Alignment (Fitting a shorter sequence into a longer one)
     * Repeat Finder


   Standalone Tools
     * PubChemSR v1.9(Sep. 8, 2006)
         (Windows-based application for searching & downloading data from NCBI's PubChem Chemical databases)
          Demonstration Video(More on the PubChemSR homepage)

   Tools at ISTECH Inc.
     * GOODIES
         (Biological interpretation program for Microarray clustering results based on Gene-Ontology)
     * IDPKP
          (An integrated database for protein kinases and proteases)
