    7. Project : An energy based motif finding algorithm using chromatin immuniprecipitation on microarray (ChIP-on-Chip)

Abstract

Finding motifs is an important challenge in computational biology. This paper describes an improved Energy-Based Motif Finding (EBMF) algorithm employing the chromatin immunoprecipitation on microarray (ChIP-on-Chip) experiments data. Unlike most other computational methods for motif discovery, based on the strong-signal model, the EBMF algorithm can utilize additional weak-signal information from the ChIP-on-Chip data. Thus it shows successful performance even in cases other popular methods fail. Our goal is to improve the currently available EBMF algorithm by incorporating association information between positions in a motif and by allowing the length of target motif not to be fixed. Up to now, we have implement basic features of the EBMF and will continue to incorporate other features.
