(E) Functions : 
    - Basic molecular function of shaggy is kinase activity which 
      phosphorylates its target proteins.
         ATP + a protein = ADP + a phosphoprotein

    - It works at glycogen synthesis pathway via insulin-mediated inhibition.

    - It is required for several developmental events such as synctial 
      blastoderm formation and embryonic segmentation. 

    - Depending on its presence in the cells, it regulates 
      * Dorsal-ventral axes in vertebrates
      * anterior-posterior segment polarity in Drosophila
      * animal-vegetal patterns in sea urchin
      * endoderm/mesoderm cell fates in C. elegans

    - Targets various substrates. For example, beta-catenin is phosphorylated
      by shaggy protein and then it is degraded by ubiqutous proteases. 

    - It is involved in transcriptional regulation and it's know to be 
      required for Arm phosphorylation. 

    - Wg signals operates by inactivating the Sgg repression of autoactivation

    - More details are provided in a separate report, Renaissance_of_GSK3.txt

    (2) Phenotype : 
        Loss-of-function (LOF) mutation in the shaggy protein (GSK3) has been
        reported. The phenotype for this mutation in Drosophila is a failure 
        to properly differentiate segment polarity during embryogenesis.  
        Normal segment polarity establishes alternating bands of naked cuticle
        and bristly denticle belts.  In shaggy mutants, the denticle belts 
        are eliminated.  

	Protein kinase GSK3 participates in a number of morphogenic and 
	regulatory metabolic pathways, and well-conserved homologs 
	have been characterized in Xenopus, mouse, C. elegans, Dictyostelium,
	and human systems, among others.  Mutations in this gene generally 
	result in abnormal embryogenesis due to its pivotal role 
	in mediating cell fate.

	GKS3 is also involved in the Drosophila circadian locomotor activity 
	cycle, with overexpression leading to a shortening of this period.  
	Inhibition of GSK3 activity has been shown to retard apoptosis 
	in neuronal cell cultures.  GSK3¡¯s role as a deactivator of 
	glycogen synthase has led to investigations of its relationship 
	to diabetes, as well.  Diabetic and obese strains of mice have been 
	shown to have elevated levels of GSK3, and cell-permeant GSK3 
	inhibitors are expected to mimic some of the activity of insulin.

(2) Selected Members of Protein Kinase Family.

 From the Pfam flatfile (Pfam-A.full.gz), we extracted the 'PF00069' record,
 which has 10,011 protein members. Instead of just taking out a certain
 number of proteins, we selected only the proteins that showed 
 a high sequence similarity to the Fruitfly shaggy protein. 
 The similarities are based on a BLAST search against UniProt Database.
 We succesfully selected 435 protein sequences (kinase domains) and 
 aligned them by using ClustalW. Result files are as follows
 
  KinaseFamily.aln : Alignment of Domain sequences by ClustalW
  KinaseFamily.fas : Alignment of Domain sequences in the FASTA format
 
(3) Selected Possible Orthologous Proteins
 Possible orthologous protein sequences have been aligned and saved
 into both aln and FASTA files. To illustrate differences and 
 similarities within shaggy protein isoforms, all isoform sequences
 from Swiss-prot were included. Result files are as follows
 
  OrthologFull.aln : Alignment of FULL protein sequences by ClustalW
  OrthologFull.fas : Alignment of FULL protein sequences in the FASTA format
  OrthologDomain.aln : Alignment of domain sequences by ClustalW
  OrthologDomain.fas : Alignment of domain sequences in the FASTA format
