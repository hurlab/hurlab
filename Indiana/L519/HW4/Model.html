LOD Calculator for Sequence Models


Hello! Welcome to Junguk's LOD Calculator.Here you can check to which sequence model(Ms1, Ms2) your data sequence(d) more likely belongs. Calculation of LOD score of a sequence is based upon the following expression
       exp(L ( D | Ms1 ) - L ( D | Ms2))
If the log odds ratio is greater than 0, it means the data sequence is more like to the sequence Model#1. If it is less than 0, the data sequence is more like to the sequence Model#2. If it's exactly o, the data sequence is eqally likely from both models.

Please enter three sequences (FASTA format is also accepted) SEQ#1 (MODEL1), SEQ#2 (MODEL2), DATA (To be analyzed).
Both protein and nucleotide sequences are allowed Click 'SampleSet' button for sample sequence inputs.
